How much do Facebook ads cost for schools & academies in Pakistan?

By the Enrollners teamUpdated 31 October 20266 min read
The short answer

In our own education campaigns in Pakistan, Facebook and Instagram ads have cost about Rs 15 to Rs 20 per WhatsApp conversation and about Rs 3,400 per lead-form lead for a higher-fee programme. There is no fixed price: Meta runs an auction, so your cost depends on your audience, city, timing, creative and offer. The number that matters is not cost per click but cost per enrolled student, and you can work out what you can afford from your fee.

Why a conversation costs less than a lead

A WhatsApp conversation is a light first step: someone taps a button and sends a message. A lead form asks for more information, so fewer people complete it and each one costs more, but they are often further along in their decision. Neither is better on its own. What matters is how many of each become students, which is why we track every lead to enrolment.

How to work out your budget

Start from your fee and work backwards:

  • Maximum cost per lead = fee × share of leads who enrol × share of the fee you are willing to spend
  • Example: Rs 60,000 fee × 10% of leads enrol × 10% of fee = Rs 600 per lead
  • Monthly budget = students you want × number of leads needed per student × cost per lead
  • Example: 20 students × 10 leads each × Rs 200 per lead = Rs 40,000

If your actual cost per lead comes in below your maximum, you can safely spend more. If it is above, the fix is usually the offer, the creative or the follow-up, not just the budget.

What makes education ads more expensive

  • Targeting a very small area or a very narrow audience
  • Starting late, when every institution in your city is advertising at once
  • Generic creative, such as a building photo and “Admissions open”
  • Sending people to a slow website instead of WhatsApp or a short form
  • Optimising for clicks or likes instead of conversations or leads
  • Changing campaigns every day, so Meta never learns who responds

How to lower your cost per admission

The cheapest way to lower cost per admission is often outside the ad: reply faster, follow up more, and make the visit or demo class easy to book. On the ad side, use real photos and short videos, answer a specific question in each ad, test a few versions and keep the winners running. How much does a Facebook ad cost in Pakistan?

There is no fixed price because Meta runs an auction. In our education campaigns, WhatsApp conversations have cost about Rs 15 to Rs 20 each and lead-form leads for a higher-fee programme about Rs 3,400 each. Your cost depends on audience, city, timing and creative.

What is the minimum budget for school ads?

Meta lets you start with a small daily budget. A realistic test is a few weeks at a budget you are comfortable with, enough to see cost per lead and cost per admission before scaling up.

Are Instagram ads cheaper than Facebook ads?

Not necessarily. Most campaigns run on both through Meta, which places ads wherever results are cheapest. Instagram tends to reach students, Facebook more parents.

Is it better to pay per click or per message?

For most schools and academies in Pakistan, campaigns optimised for WhatsApp conversations or leads work better than paying for clicks, because they reach people more likely to actually contact you.

How can I reduce my cost per admission?

Reply within five minutes, follow up every inquiry, use real photos and videos, target parents or students near you, and start campaigns before the season rather than during it.

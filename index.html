What does an education marketing agency do?

An education marketing agency helps schools, coaching centres, colleges and training institutes attract and enrol students. Enrollners plans admission campaigns on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google, replies to every inquiry on WhatsApp within minutes, tracks each lead in a CRM until it becomes an admission, and reports your cost per enrolled student every month.

How can a private school in Pakistan get more admissions?

Start before admission season (February to April for most private schools), show parents what they actually worry about (safety, teachers, results and fees), make it easy to book a campus visit online, reply on WhatsApp within minutes, and follow up every inquiry until the admission form is in. Most schools lose more students to slow follow-up than to weak ads.

How much should a school or academy spend on Facebook and Instagram ads?

Work it out from your fee: fee × the share of leads who enrol × the share of the fee you are happy to spend = the most you can pay per lead. For example, with a Rs 60,000 fee, 1 in 10 leads enrolling and 10% of the fee as your limit, up to Rs 600 per lead is fine. Start with a small daily budget and scale the ads that bring admissions, not just clicks.

What is a good cost per lead for education ads in Pakistan?

It depends on your fee and how many leads become students. In our own education campaigns, WhatsApp conversations have cost about Rs 15 to Rs 20 each, while lead forms for a higher-fee programme cost about Rs 3,400 per lead. The number that matters most is cost per enrolled student, which we report every month.

How fast should an institute reply to a new inquiry?

Within five minutes. Students and parents usually message three or four institutes at once and join the one that answers well first. An instant WhatsApp greeting, quick replies for fees, timings and batches, and an alert to a counsellor the moment a lead arrives make that possible without hiring more staff.

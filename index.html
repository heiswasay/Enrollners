— The problem

Most agencies sell likes. You need enrolled students.

Every inquiry you pay for is a student who raised their hand. When nobody replies for a day, they join the academy or school that replied in five minutes. That's a seat you paid for and handed to a competitor.

Campaigns miss the intake window

Ads start late, stop early, or run the same message all year while deadlines come and go.

Leads go cold before anyone replies

Inquiries sit in a spreadsheet or an unread inbox. By the time a counsellor calls, the student has joined someone else.

Reports count reach, not seats

Impressions and followers look good in a PDF. What you need to know is what each enrolled student cost.

— Sound familiar?

The three problems we fix most often.

If one of these sounds like your institute, the fix is usually faster than you think.

Training institutes

“We get leads, but they’re the wrong students.”

Broad targeting and a one-line form attract people who were never going to enrol. Your team spends the day on calls that go nowhere.

How we fix it
  • Targeting by age, education level and budget signals
  • Qualifying questions in the form: level, timing, budget
  • Serious leads sent to your team on WhatsApp instantly
Coaching & test-prep academies

“Our ads worked for a month, then stopped.”

The same creative runs until people stop noticing it, and one generic page tries to sell every course to every student.

How we fix it
  • New creative every week, written for each batch
  • A landing page per course and per city
  • Budget moved daily to the ads that bring admissions
Private schools

“Parents see our ads but never visit.”

School ads that talk about facilities don't answer what parents actually worry about, and there's no easy way to book a visit.

How we fix it
  • Parent-focused campaigns from February to April
  • Online campus-visit booking with WhatsApp reminders
  • Follow-up sequence until the admission form is in
